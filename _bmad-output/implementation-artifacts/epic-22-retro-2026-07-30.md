## What Went Well

1. **The 22.1 spike gated real re-scopes with real numbers, not theoretical ones.** It measured `RegenerateEpics` producing a 56-page work-graph over-count at *no-op* — a live defect in shipped `watch` mode, independent of the IR pivot — and used that to mandate 22.5 be treated as a correctness story rather than a performance one.
2. **A gate resolved itself before the gated story even started.** 22.4's source-derived `FollowUpRefs.BuildHrefMap` fix — built to close an unrelated 46-page static/IR divergence — turned out to be the exact root cause of the correctness defect 22.1 had mandated 22.5 fix. By the time 22.5 opened, re-measurement against the byte-parity oracle showed the no-op case was already byte-identical; nothing needed moving.
3. **The team caught its own harness's false positive before trusting it.** 22.6's delta-measurement gate first reported a suspicious 0.000% — because the edit it measured touched an ignored dotfile and the watch route silently returned `Skipped`, so it was measuring a delta of nothing against everything. Liveness (dispatched as expected, pages actually changed) was added to the gate itself before the real PASS was accepted.
4. **Golden-fingerprint causality was correctly isolated twice**, exactly per CLAUDE.md's rule to establish causality before regenerating: 22.4 separated its own region-wrapper move from a concurrent code review's edits; 22.6 distinguished its own rule's byte impact (`501ee958…`) from a concurrent session's unrelated drift (`078ef476…`) in `specscribe.css`.
5. **Live-browser verification caught a real concurrency race.** 22.6's topology degrade-to-full signal was first derived from a `trigger` label; a concurrent session's save overwrote that label between it being set and read, producing a sidecar/log mismatch invisible to the test suite. Caught and fixed live, and re-verified live under the identical race — the exact pattern CLAUDE.md's Verification section predicts.

## Challenges

1. **ADR slot-number guessing failed every single time this epic, and the miss margin grew.** Every Epic 22 story guessed its own "next free" ADR number at authoring time, and every guess was stale by ratification: 22.2 guessed 0023 (a concurrent session's Story 25.3 took it), 22.4 guessed 0023 too (landed on 0024), 22.5 guessed 0024 (landed on 0027 — 22.4 had taken 0024), 22.6 guessed 0024 again (landed on 0028 — four ADRs landed in between). This is a distinct failure mode from the standing ADR-*consultation* action item (which is about citing the right ADR's *content*, not predicting its *number*) — concurrency makes any number written before ratification unreliable.
2. **Epic 22 and Epic 23 functioned as one continuous stream of work split across two epic numbers.** Story 22.3 was retired mid-epic and replaced by a different epic's story (23.4); 22.4's scope inherited two defects handed back from 23.3; owner sequencing decisions repeatedly reordered stories across the epic boundary (22.4 before 23.4; 22.6 before 22.5). This retro is running well after Epic 23 already shipped 23.1 and put 23.2/23.3/23.5 through review — the same "these should have been one retro" shape the Epic 19+21 joint retro named.
3. **Standing tax, unchanged:** golden-fingerprint regeneration churn continues, accepted per the last two retros.

## Key Takeaways

1. A spike's mandated gate can resolve itself before the gated story starts, if an earlier sibling story happens to share the same root cause — worth re-measuring against the oracle before assuming a mandated fix is still needed.
2. Distrust a measurement harness's PASS as much as its FAIL: 22.6's 0.000% would have shipped a false confidence claim had liveness not been added to the gate.
3. ADR number-guessing in story files is now failing on every guess under this project's concurrency level. **Owner decision this retro: accepted as a cost of concurrency, no remedy pursued** — same disposition as the golden-fingerprint tax.
4. Epics that are logically one continuous piece of work (22 → 23) are a recurring shape (this is the second time, after 19+21). **Owner decision this retro: keep one-retrospective-per-epic as the default given the tooling, but keep the option open to combine entangled epics when it clearly fits** — not a standing rule change, a preference to have in the toolkit.
