  Root cause, verified in `SiteGenerator.cs`: the dashboard/epics families are RE-RENDERED for the
  IR (`BuildSpaBundle`, :3044) rather than captured from the static pass's own output. The two
  passes run at different points in the pipeline and therefore see different work inventories:

                follow-up inventory straight from source (`ResolveFollowUpWork(files)`) and passes
                an explicitly parsed deferred model.
    · IR      — `BuildSpaBundle` :3053 runs AFTER, so `WorkInventory.Build(_docs)` sees MORE items,
                and the 2-arg `BuildFollowUpGeometry` re-derives the deferred model itself.
